Pepi & Noco

A 3D cartoon for young kids about Pepi, a little white monkey, and Noco, a big purple iguana.

Each episode is a short, gentle story told by a narrator who talks with the child, with a simple lesson at the end (asking for help, sharing, friendship). The show is made in three languages:

  • Português: youtube.com/@pepienoco
  • English: youtube.com/@pepiandnoco
  • Español: Pepi y Noco

This site also hosts the private publishing tool we use to upload and schedule our own episodes on our own YouTube channels.
